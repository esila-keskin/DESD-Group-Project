# Functionality 2

Group 51, Bristol Regional Food Network (Use Case 2). Questions about the payment process and the simulated payment service, along with suggested solutions for each.

# Payment details

**Q1:** What Information is sent to the simulated service?<br>
**Suggestion:** The customer's name and card details (fictitious test data in this case) are passed on to be verified by the provider and process the transaction.

# Declined payments

**Q2:** What does the customer see after a declined payment?<br>
**Suggestion:** The customer should remain at checkout and be shown a message explaining that an issue occurred during the transaction. They should have an extra opportunity to check/ammend their card details and try again.

# Retry attempts

**Q3:** How many retry attempts are permitted after a failed payment?<br>
**Suggestion:** Not yet agreed upon, initial suggestion is 2 attempts

# Order Confirmation

**Q4:** Once payment is successful and the order confirmed, how is this shared with the customer?<br>
**Suggestion:** After checking out, the customer should be met with a message telling them that their order has been confirmed and a confirmation email could be sent as an extra measure to keep the customer informed and act as a personal record of their order for them.
